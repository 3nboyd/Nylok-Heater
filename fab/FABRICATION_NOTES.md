# Fabrication notes

Board outline: 38 mm x 100 mm, nominal 1.6 mm FR-4, 2 copper layers.

This reconstruction is not released for fabrication. Critical items requiring KiCad validation and electrical review include the MP8771 implementation, exact vendor pin mapping, power-stage current path, gate-drive details, shunt Kelvin routing, trace widths, copper weight, thermal relief, EMI, BMS/fuse/master-switch integration, and external ZVS compatibility.
